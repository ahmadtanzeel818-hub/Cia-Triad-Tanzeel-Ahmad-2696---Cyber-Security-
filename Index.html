Confidentiality, Integrity & Availability

The foundational model guiding information security policies, risk assessments, and defensive architecture across ISO/IEC 27001 and NIST frameworks.

🔒 Confidentiality

Preventing unauthorized disclosure. Ensures sensitive data is accessible only to authorized users, processes, or systems via encryption, authentication, and least-privilege access control.

🛡️ Integrity

Preventing unauthorized alteration. Guarantees data remains accurate, trustworthy, and untampered throughout its lifecycle using cryptographic hashing, digital signatures, and audit logs.

⚡ Availability

Preventing unauthorized disruption. Ensures systems, networks, and data remain reliably accessible to authorized users when needed through redundancy, failover, and DDoS mitigation.

CIA Triad vs. DAD Triad (The Threat Counterparts)

Every protective pillar in the CIA model directly counters an adversarial failure mode in the DAD model.

CIA Pillar (Defense) DAD Counterpart (Threat) What Goes Wrong Primary Mitigation
Confidentiality Disclosure Unauthorized parties view, steal, or leak private records, credentials, or intellectual property. AES-256 / TLS 1.3 encryption, RBAC, MFA, Data Loss Prevention (DLP).
Integrity Alteration Data or software is maliciously modified, corrupted in transit, or injected with backdoors. SHA-256 hashing, HMAC, digital signatures, immutable append-only logs.
Availability Destruction / Denial Critical servers crash, files are wiped/ransomed, or legitimate users are locked out of services. Load balancing, multi-region failover, 3-2-1 backups, DDoS rate limiting.
